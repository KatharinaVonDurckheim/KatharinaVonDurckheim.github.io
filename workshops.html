Advanced Animal Movement Modelling — 16-17 September 2026, Stellenbosch University

A two-day advanced training workshop covering telemetry fundamentals, data quality, home-range estimation, behavioural-state modelling, habitat selection, connectivity and reproducible workflows in R.

Hosts: Stellenbosch University, Wildlife Free to Roam and NiTHeCS

Quantitative ecology training

Movement ecology methods and R workflows

Training resources focus on quantitative approaches for analysing animal movement and habitat use, with an emphasis on transparent, reproducible analytical workflows.

Topics include movement trajectories, home-range estimation, behavioural-state models, habitat-selection analyses, connectivity and spatial ecological modelling.
