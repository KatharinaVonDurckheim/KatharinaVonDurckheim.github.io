Animal movement and spatial ecology

Advanced Animal Movement Modelling — Stellenbosch, September 2026

A two-day advanced training workshop in contemporary animal movement ecology, combining conceptual lectures, practical coding sessions and collaborative research discussions using African wildlife telemetry data.

The workshop covers movement data preparation, home-range estimation, behavioural-state modelling, habitat selection, connectivity and reproducible movement-ecology workflows in R.

Dates: 16–17 September 2026
Location: Stellenbosch University, South Africa
Lead facilitator: Dr Josh Cullen, University of California Santa Cruz
Hosts: Stellenbosch University, Wildlife Free to Roam and NiTHeCS

Workshop details & programme Workshop materials & code

Workshop information and the programme are available through NiTHeCS. Scripts, example data and live-coding materials are maintained in the workshop GitHub repository.

Note: the GitHub workshop repository is currently private and accessible only to authorised collaborators. The link will become publicly accessible if the repository is made public.

Quantitative ecology training

Movement ecology methods and R workflows

Training focuses on quantitative approaches for analysing animal movement and habitat use, with an emphasis on transparent and reproducible analytical workflows.

Topics include movement trajectories, home-range estimation, behavioural-state models, habitat-selection analyses, connectivity and spatial ecological modelling.

Training resources

Workshop slides, example datasets, R scripts and supporting materials will be made available through the Resources page and associated GitHub repositories.
