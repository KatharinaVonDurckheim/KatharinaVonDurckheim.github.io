Animal movement and spatial ecology

Advanced Animal Movement Modelling — Stellenbosch University, 16–17 September 2026

A two-day advanced training workshop in contemporary animal movement ecology, developed and taught by Dr Josh Cullen (UC Santa Cruz) and hosted at Stellenbosch University. The workshop combined conceptual lectures, practical coding sessions and collaborative research discussions using African wildlife telemetry data.

Training covered movement-data preparation and reproducible workflows in R, including autocorrelated kernel density estimation (AKDE), dynamic Brownian bridge movement models (dBBMM), resource selection functions (RSF), step and integrated step-selection functions (SSF/iSSF), hidden Markov models (HMM), and Bayesian spatial modelling approaches including INLA.

Dates: 16–17 September 2026
Location: Stellenbosch University
Workshop instructor: Dr Josh Cullen, UC Santa Cruz
Hosts: Stellenbosch University, Wildlife Free to Roam and NiTHeCS
